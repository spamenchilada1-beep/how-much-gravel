Gravel Driveway Calculator Guide

Driveway gravel is usually estimated from the area to cover, the depth of the material, and the density of the selected gravel. Driveway construction may also involve multiple layers rather than one uniform depth.

Start with the gravel calculator

Enter your driveway dimensions and depth in the ATHENA Gravel Calculator to estimate cubic yards and approximate tons.

Measure the driveway

For a rectangular area, multiply length by width to get square feet. For irregular areas, break the driveway into manageable sections and calculate each section separately.

Convert depth to feet

Because cubic-yard calculations use feet, divide the depth in inches by 12 before calculating volume.

Cubic yards = length × width × depth in feet ÷ 27

Why driveway quantities vary

Driveway projects can use different stone sizes and multiple base and surface layers. Material density also varies, so the same cubic-yard volume can have different weights.

Estimate tons carefully

The calculator's tonnage is an estimate based on the material assumptions used by the tool. Confirm the supplier's density or delivered quantity before ordering a large load.

Allow for site conditions

Uneven ground, compaction, grading, spillage, and the actual finished depth can change the amount required. A contractor or material supplier can help confirm the final order quantity for a specific driveway design.
