How Much Gravel Do I Need?

Estimate the amount of gravel needed for driveways, walkways, patios, drainage areas, and landscape beds. Enter the area and depth to calculate cubic yards, tons, and a practical order quantity.

Gravel density varies by material, so treat tonnage as an estimate and verify the supplier's weight-per-yard information before ordering. Depth and compaction can also affect the final quantity.
